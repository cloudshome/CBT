## 1. Executive Abstract
In-depth analysis of the secular economic divergence between the United States and the People's Republic of China. Deconstructs the collision between US financialized debt dynamics (surging debt service costs, fiscal deficits) and China's physical manufacturing overcapacity, real-economy supply dominance, and transition toward multi-polar trade rails.

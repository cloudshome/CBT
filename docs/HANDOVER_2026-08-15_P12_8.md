## 2. The gap

P12.6 restated the desk's numbers net of costs:
`E_net = E_gross − fees − slip − funding`. Every reporting surface followed —
the scorecard, the quality score, the strategy score, the Monte Carlo table.
The bar itself was restated net.

But a measurement only matters where something acts on it. Two sites act:

| site | anchor it read | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| `data.strategy_score.multiplier_from_score()` | `E_gross` | scaled engine confidence up or down |
| `brain.risk_gate.setup_proven()` (A6/B10) | `backtest_expectancy`, `paper_expectancy` (gross) | decided which setups may be traded at all |

Both were still reading gross. The project was measuring with one number and
deciding with another.

### The measured consequence

Constructed from the repo's own cost model, not hypothesised:

```
E_gross = +0.055R      (positive — looks like an edge)
E_net    = −0.118R     (negative — loses money once fees are paid)
multiplier = ×1.008    (the engine RAISES confidence on it)
```

A setup that loses money was having its confidence boosted. Not by much —
0.8% — but in the wrong direction, and the sign is what matters. The same
book could also clear `setup_proven()`, because that gate compared gross
expectancy against zero.

This is exactly the failure mode `PATH_TO_REAL_MONEY.md` §2 warned about
("costs are not in the number"), surviving in the two places the document
did not check.

## 3. What shipped

- **`data/strategy_score.py`** — `multiplier_from_score()` takes
  `expectancy_net` and anchors its sign on it, falling back to gross only
  when costs could not be computed. The case above becomes **×0.982**.
- **`brain/calibrator.py`** — `build_profile()` passes the net anchor; the
  `CALIBRATE_FILTER` negative-expectancy filter judges net too; `describe()`
  states which anchor is in use.
- **`brain/risk_gate.py`** — `setup_proven()` requires positive net
  expectancy where available and reports `proven_on: "net" | "gross"`. Its
  block message now names the failing condition; previously a
  net-expectancy rejection printed only sample counts and read like a bug.
- **`data/database.py`** — `setup_stats()` returns
  `backtest_expectancy_net` / `paper_expectancy_net` from the stored
  `rr_net` column, or `None` when the setup has no net values.
- **`tests/test_net_anchor.py`** — 11 tests, including a **monotonicity
  property**: for a range of expectancies, the net anchor can only lower a
  multiplier, never raise one. That property is what makes the change safe
  to reason about without re-deriving every case.

### Why this is safe

The change is **strictly tighter, never looser**. It can only dampen a
multiplier or withdraw a "proven" — never the reverse. The graduation bar,
the sample floors (100 backtest / 20 paper), and every read-only guarantee
are untouched. Where `rr_net` is absent — old rows, costs disabled —
behaviour is byte-identical to before, so historical books do not silently
change meaning.

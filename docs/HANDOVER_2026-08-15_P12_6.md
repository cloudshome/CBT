## 1. What shipped

### A. Trade Cost Engine (`data/costs.py`)
- **Equation**: $E_{\text{net}} = E_{\text{gross}} − \text{fees} − \text{slip} − \text{funding}$
- **`CostModel`**: configurable fee schedules (default taker 0.05%/side = 0.10% round-trip, maker 0.02%/side, spot 0.10%/side), symbol-specific spreads (BTC 0.02% half-spread, ETH 0.035%, XAU 0.06%), slippage modeling, and standard 8h funding intervals.
- **`calculate_trade_cost()`**: computes `fee_r`, `slip_r`, `funding_r`, `total_cost_r`, and `net_r` scaled to the trade's planned 1R stop distance ($|entry - sl| / entry$).
- **Funding as-of-T**: direction-aware funding (`get_funding_rate_as_of_t`) looked up from stored derivatives series as of signal timestamp $T$ (long pays positive funding, short receives positive funding credit; spot has 0 funding).

### B. Grader & Database Persistence
- `data/backtester.py`: `GradedPlan` and `_evaluate()` compute and attach `rr_net`, `fee_r`, `slip_r`, `funding_r`, `cost_r`.
- `data/database.py`: additive migrations add `rr_net`, `fee_r`, `slip_r`, `funding_r`, `cost_r` to `backtest_results` and `paper_trades`. `save_backtest_rows()` persists all net metrics.

### C. Scorecard & Net Graduation Bar
- `data/scorecard.py`: `trade_stats()` and `build_scorecard()` calculate both gross and net performance metrics (`expectancy_net`, `win_rate_net`, `pf_net`, `total_r_net`, `max_drawdown_r_net`, `sharpe_net`, `sortino_net`, `calmar_net`, `cost_r`, `fee_r`, `slip_r`, `funding_r`).
- **Conditioned splits**: `conditioned_splits()` evaluates expectancy partitioned by funding regime (POSITIVE, NEUTRAL, NEGATIVE) and CVD order flow (BULLISH_FLOW, NEUTRAL_FLOW, BEARISH_FLOW).
- **Net Bar Enforcement**: `meets_bar()` in `data/validation.py` and `build_scorecard()` evaluate against the restated **NET** bar: $E_{\text{net}} \ge +0.50\text{R}$, $WR_{\text{net}} > 55\%$, $PF_{\text{net}} \ge 1.5$.
- `data/simulator.py`: `graduation_status` and `format_graduation` report net metrics across primary setup families.

### D. Surfaces (CLI / HTTP / MCP / UI)
- CLI: `python main.py scorecard [--no-costs] [--maker] [--conditioned] [--json]`
- HTTP: `GET /api/scorecard` supports `no_costs`, `conditioned`, `since`, `until`.
- Dashboard: Learning card displays Net Expectancy, Gross Expectancy, Net WR, Net PF, and Cost Drag breakdown.
- MCP Server: `scorecard` tool updated to v1.6.0 with `no_costs` and `conditioned` arguments.

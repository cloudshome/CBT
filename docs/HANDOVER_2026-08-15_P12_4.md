## 1. The problem this closes

The scorecard (P12.3/P12.6/P12.3.3) answers *"is this book profitable net
of costs, with honest overlap-corrected uncertainty?"* — but a desk cannot
risk money on "profitable" alone. A book whose entire PnL comes from five
lucky trades, or from one lucky month, or from one isolated parameter
point, *looks* like an edge in the mean and is nothing of the sort.

P12.4 is the second question:

> **"Is this book robust, or did it win by luck?"**

Per `docs/FREQTRADE_REVIEW.md` §8 F5 and `docs/BACKTRADER_REVIEW.md` §8
BT5, it is a composite 0–100 so a fragile high-PnL curve cannot look like
a pass — now scoring the **real** (net-of-costs, overlap-corrected) data
that P12.6 and P12.3.3 produced.

## 2. What shipped

### A. `data/quality_score.py` — the pure score

`backtest_quality_score(rows, *, min_decided, criteria, step, plateau=None)`
→ one dict with `quality` (0–100), `band`, `weights`, `components`,
`plateau_cap`. Four weighted components, each 0–100 (renormalised when the
plateau evidence is absent):

| component | weight | what it measures |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **edge** | 35% | net expectancy vs the bar: +0.50R → 50, 2× bar → 100; ×0.85 per miss when WR_net ≤ 55% or PF_net < 1.5 — the bar is all three conditions |
| **robustness** | 35% | fragility of the cumulative net-R curve: top-5 wins' share of gross wins (40%), max-DD ÷ \|total R\| (30%), chronological segment consistency — 4 chunks when n ≥ 12, 2 when n ≥ 4, n/a below (30%) |
| **sample** | 20% | N_eff vs min_decided, plus where the 95% CI of E_net sits relative to break-even (CI low ≥ 0 → 100; ≤ −bar → 0) |
| **plateau** | 10% | the BT5 parameter-plateau test: fraction of neighbourhood points with E_net > 0 (70%) × mean E_net vs bar (30%) |

**The cap:** an isolated peak (plateau < 50) caps the total at
`PLATEAU_CAP = 50` and sets `plateau_cap: True` — a fit, not an edge.

Bands: ≥ 80 `robust` · ≥ 60 `acceptable` · ≥ 40 `borderline` · else `fragile`.
Empty books degrade to `quality: None` with a reason (silence is not an edge).

### B. `python main.py quality` — the plateau sweep (store-only)

`run_quality_sweep()` re-grades the stored series at `min_confidence ± 5`
(clamped [40, 90], deduplicated; the **base point is guaranteed** even when
an offset clamps onto it), then scores the base book with that evidence.
Store-only by design, exactly like the locked P12.1 protocol; `grade_fn`
is injectable so CI stays offline.

### C. One computation, many surfaces (invariant #10)

- `python main.py scorecard --quality` — embeds the block (edge /
  robustness / sample; "plateau n/a" without the re-grade sweep).
- `GET /api/scorecard?quality=1` and the `STRATEGY SCORECARD` dashboard
  card render the same block.
- MCP `scorecard` tool gained a `quality` argument (SERVER_VERSION 1.8.0).

## 1. Finding — the live risk controls still pool every source

P12.13 isolated graduation *performance* evidence to the canonical live
book; P12.14 did the same for the *discipline* criterion. The safety review
of the merged tree asked the obvious next question: **what else makes a
decision from `paper_trades` without a provenance filter?**

Answer: every live *risk control*. They all read

```python
rows = db.decided_paper_rows(exclude_sim=True)   # no source filter
```

which excludes simulator walk-forward rows (`sim_key IS NOT NULL`) but
applies **no** `source_key` clause — the P12.11 boundary never reached them:

| control | site | direction of failure |
|---|---|---|
| daily/weekly loss limits | `brain/risk_gate.py closed_pnl()` | research wins offset real losses → fails **open**; research losses trip the limit → false block |
| drawdown ladder | `brain/risk_gate.py drawdown()` | research wins refill a real losing streak → fails **open** |
| Kelly advisory | `risk_gate.evaluate()`, `trading_intelligence` | dead (see below), and unisolated by design of the call |
| MC ruin table (`/api/monte`, `/api/risk`, MCP, dashboard Risk card) | `brain/analytics.py monte_carlo_ruin_table()` | "live-book risk read" resamples research outcomes |
| MC equity, MAE/MFE review | `brain/analytics.py` | same population |
| business scorecard, equity curve | `brain/metrics.py` | same population |

A second, independent defect fell out of reading the Kelly call site:

```python
kelly = kelly_from_progress(db.decided_paper_rows(exclude_sim=True))
```

`kelly_from_progress()` expects **`paper_progress()` aggregates** (rows with
`wins` / `win_r` keys), not raw trade rows. On any non-empty book it raises
`KeyError('wins')`, the blanket `except` converts that into
`{"note": "unavailable"}`, and the dashboard Kelly card (which hides when
`kelly_size == null`) has therefore **never rendered with data**. There are
no tests on either call site.

Today's writers set `sim_key` on every research row, so `exclude_sim`
happens to shield the controls — but nothing *enforces* that, the schema
permits real (`sim_key IS NULL`) non-canonical rows, and P12.14's own probe
constructs them. The whole point of the Dukascopy track is that research
books will keep growing; the risk gate must not depend on an accident of
today's writer set. This is the same standard P12.13/P12.14 were held to.


Observed on `fdfc9f4` (student progression: risk 0.25%/trade, daily limit
1.0%, weekly 2.0%; ladder 5/8/10%):

| Case | Book actually present | Pooled read on `fdfc9f4` | Correct answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** masked loss limit | 5 real canonical losses today (−1.25%) + 6 research wins | `r_sum +7.0R` → **not blocked** | daily limit **blocked** |
| **B** masked drawdown | 24-loss canonical streak (6.0% dd) interleaved with research wins | `0.25% dd / normal` | `6.0% / reduce_risk` |
| **C** research-only book | 8 research losses, zero live trades | daily limit **blocked**; MC ruin table `available` over 8 trades; business scorecard `n=8` | nothing moves: not blocked, MC unavailable, `n=0` |
| **D** Kelly advisory | any populated book | `KeyError('wins')` → `{"note": "unavailable"}` | a real, canonical-only advisory |
| **E** legacy NULL rows | 5 canonical losses saved before `source_key` existed | blocked (correct) | blocked — must stay counted |
| **F** control | 3W/2L canonical-only book | `+4.0R`, not blocked | identical before and after |

Case F is the regression anchor: a canonical-only book must keep producing
exactly the numbers it produces today.

## 3. Implementation contract (P12.15 / v2.23.3)

One population definition, many surfaces (invariant #10):

1. **`SignalDB.live_book_rows(since_ts=None, symbol=None)`** — THE live
   paper book: decided (`TP_HIT`/`STOP_LOSS`), `sim_key IS NULL`,
   canonical-live (`COALESCE(source_key,'')=''`; legacy NULL provenance rows
   are the canonical series and stay counted). Implemented as a thin
   delegation to `decided_paper_rows(..., exclude_sim=True, source_key="")`
   so the P12.11 `_source_clause` algebra is reused, not re-derived.
2. Every live risk control reads it: `closed_pnl()`, `drawdown()`, the two
   Kelly call sites, `monte_carlo_ruin_table()`, `monte_carlo_equity()`,
   `mae_mfe_summary()`, `business_metrics()` / `_equity_curve()`.
3. **`engine.hidden_alpha.kelly_from_rows(rows)`** replaces the broken
   `kelly_from_progress(raw rows)` calls: accepts trade rows, judges NET of
   costs where `rr_net` exists (P12.8; gross fallback), classifies win/loss
   by net sign (the MC resampler's rule), stands aside at 0.0% on a book
   with no net winners, suggests nothing on an empty book, and emits the
   keys the surfaces read (`kelly_size`, `expectancy`, `n_trades` plus the
   `kelly_from_stats` payload). Advisory only; the existing
   `KELLY_MAX_RISK_PCT` clamp at the call sites is unchanged.
4. **Not** touched: `decided_paper_rows()` signature/behaviour for explicit
   callers; the scorecard research surfaces (`rows_from_paper_db` with
   `include_sim` / `source_key`, `by_source` splits); every threshold, cap
   and ladder rung; `kelly_from_progress()` itself (still correct for
   progress aggregates); the graduation gate (P12.13/P12.14 payloads).

## 4. What this changes for the desk

* A losing day on the real canonical book now always closes the desk at the
  limit, no matter what any research book did.
* A real losing streak now always walks the drawdown ladder.
* Research books can neither block nor unlock any live control — the
  P12.13 principle, now enforced on the risk side too.
* The dashboard Kelly card renders for the first time, on canonical-live
  evidence only, net-anchored, clamped, advisory-only.
* The Risk card's MC ruin table, MAE/MFE review and business scorecard
  describe the live business only.

## 6. What was deliberately left alone

* `violation_rate()` and the journal CLI (general report — P12.14 note).
* `kelly_from_progress()` for genuine `paper_progress()` aggregates.
* The scorecard's ability to inspect any source explicitly.
* The blueprint thresholds. This build moves no bar in either direction;
  it only fixes *which book* each existing bar is measured on.

## 1. Finding

P12.15 made the business scorecard's P&L, equity curve and trade population
canonical-live-only through `SignalDB.live_book_rows()`. One nested field was
still broader: `business_metrics()["execution"]` called the intentionally
general `brain.journal.violation_rate()`, which reads every `journal_entries`
row without joining it to a decided paper trade or filtering provenance.

Therefore simulator or research journals could change the execution-violation
number displayed beside a live-only business scorecard. The overall live trade
count was correct, but its discipline sub-metric could still be pooled.

This was a reporting-boundary inconsistency, not a graduation bypass:
`graduation_discipline()` and the general `violation_rate()` contract remain
separate and unchanged.

## 2. Contract

`brain.journal.live_execution_stats(db)` is now the business scorecard's one
execution computation:

- decided (`TP_HIT` / `STOP_LOSS`) paper trades only;
- non-simulator (`sim_key IS NULL`);
- canonical-live (`COALESCE(source_key, '') = ''`, including legacy NULL rows);
- journaled `followed_rules` values determine `n`, `violations` and
  `violation_rate` with the previous journaled-entry denominator;
- eligible, journaled, missing-journal, compliant and canonical-source fields
  are also reported, so missing live journals are visible.

The broad `violation_rate(db)` function remains unchanged for the journal CLI,
general/weekly reports and historical surfaces. `graduation_discipline(db)`
remains the authoritative permission criterion and still fails closed on
missing journals.

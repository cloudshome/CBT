## 1. The problem this closes

Walk-forward grading steps `step` bars and follows each plan over
`horizon_bars` future bars, so consecutive samples overlap heavily:

| config | horizon_bars | step | overlap (`horizon_bars / step`) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 15m bars, 4h horizon | 16 | 3 | ~5.3× |
| 15m bars, 24h horizon | 96 | 3 | ~32× |

Every confidence interval and the graduation sample floor
(`min_decided`) were therefore silently understated by the overlap factor —
"384 decided" was ~12 independent observations. P12.3.3 corrects the
**counts** and the **uncertainty**, exactly as `docs/PATH_TO_REAL_MONEY.md`
§4–§5 proposed.

## 2. What shipped

### A. Overlap factor & effective sample size (`data/scorecard.py`)
- **`overlap_factor(row, default_step=BACKTEST_STEP)`** → `horizon_bars / step`
  for walk-forward backtest rows; **1.0** for paper trades (real,
  non-overlapping positions); **1.0** when horizon / timeframe / step are
  unrecorded (never invent a divisor).
- **`effective_sample_size(rows)`** → `(n_eff, overlap)` where
  `n_eff = Σ 1/overlap_i` and `overlap = n / n_eff`.
- **`trade_stats(rows, step=…)`** now reports `n_eff`, `overlap`,
  `expectancy_se`, `expectancy_ci95`, `expectancy_net_se`,
  `expectancy_net_ci95` — standard error `σ / √N_eff` and a 95% two-sided
  CI (`z = 1.96`) — on every stats block (overall and every split).

### B. The step is stored, not assumed (`data/database.py`, `main.py`)
- `backtest_results` gained a `step INTEGER` column via an additive
  migration; `save_backtest_rows()` persists it and
  `python main.py backtest --save` records `args.step`.
- `rows_from_backtest_db()` / `rows_from_graded()` now carry `horizon_hours`
  and `step` through to the row dict.

### C. Threaded through every surface
- `build_scorecard(…, step=…)`, `scorecard_from_db(…, step=…)`,
  `scorecard_from_store(…, step=…)`, `_split(…, step=…)`,
  `conditioned_splits(…, step=…)`.
- CLI `python main.py scorecard --step` now applies to the stored-book path
  too; the printed block shows `(~N_eff independent, overlap ×K)` and the
  net 95% CI, plus a `⚠` line when `N_eff < min_decided`.
- HTTP `/api/scorecard` and MCP `scorecard` (v1.7.0) inherit the new fields
  automatically (per-row `step` from the store overrides the default).

### D. The floor is flagged, not changed
- `meets_bar()` still reads **raw N** for `min_decided` (unchanged gate).
- `build_scorecard()` adds `bar.n_eff_below_min_decided` so the desk sees
  when the honest sample is thin. The floor revisit is the **next**
  decision, not this one — deliberately out of scope for P12.3.3.

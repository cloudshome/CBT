## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

Milestone 30-B establishes an **evidence-grade, point-in-time, multi-source intelligence ingestion foundation** for SKY. 

Following the institutional peer review recommendations that decomposed Milestone 30 into three strictly gated phases (M30-A Dependency Stabilization, M30-B Evidence-Grade Ingestion, M30-C Research Qualification), Milestone 30-B builds the data foundation that prevents internet rumors, revision traps, lookahead leakage, and entity confusion from polluting the downstream Milestone 28 Causal Synthesizer and Milestone 29 Walk-Forward Replay Engine.

### Core Architectural Principle: Separation of Wire Reality from Model Inference

A fatal flaw in standard LLM web scraping is treating text generated by models or aggregators as "ground truth". SKY enforces an unbreachable separation:
1. **Raw Evidence Layer (Wire Reality):** Ingested payloads (HTTP status, headers, raw response body, observed timestamp, published timestamp) are committed to an append-only, SQLite WAL-backed `RawEvidenceVault` sealed with cryptographic SHA-256 before any parser, LLM, or downstream model touches the payload.
2. **Canonical Event Identity:** Events are extracted into deterministic canonical identities (`EVENT_<TYPE>_<YYYYMMDD>_<AUTHORITY>_<DISCRIMINATOR>`) with canonical payload hashes.
3. **Entity Disambiguation:** Resolves fine-grained boundaries to prevent false cross-domain causal connections (e.g. distinguishing spot Bitcoin from MicroStrategy equity or BlackRock corporate issuer from the IBIT ETF product).
4. **Discrepancy & Revision Tracking:** Contradictory prints and official economic revisions are written to an immutable `ConflictLedger` rather than overwritten.
5. **Multi-Layer Quality Firewall:** Filters evidence across Source Quality, Evidence Integrity, and Point-in-Time Causality before reaching research synthesis.

## 3. Subsystem Breakdown

### 3.1 Source Registry (`source_registry.py`)
Enforces governance, provenance, and rate limit rules per publisher:
* **Pre-registered Sources:**
  * `src_fed_board`: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve (Officiality: 1.00, Limit: 10/min, Final on release)
  * `src_bls_gov`: Bureau of Labor Statistics (Officiality: 1.00, Limit: 10/min, Revision-prone)
  * `src_fred_stlouis`: St. Louis Fed FRED (Officiality: 0.95, Limit: 30/min, Revision-prone)
  * `src_sec_edgar`: SEC EDGAR (Officiality: 1.00, Limit: 10/min Fair Access, Append-only)
  * `src_arxiv_qfin`: arXiv.org Quantitative Finance (Officiality: 0.85, Limit: 20/min, Versioned)
  * `src_onchain_public`: Public Blockchain Node APIs (Officiality: 0.80, Limit: 30/min)
  * `src_reuters_wire`: Reuters Financial News (Officiality: 0.75, Limit: 15/min, Correction-tracked)
* **Rejection of Unregistered Sources:** Rejects unauthenticated blog scrapers and unverified sources with default conservative fallback.

### 3.2 Raw Evidence Vault (`raw_evidence_vault.py`)
* Stores all raw wire bodies prior to any text parsing, extraction, or model inference.
* Calculates SHA-256 over raw bytes: `content_sha256 = hashlib.sha256(raw_payload.encode("utf-8")).hexdigest()`.
* Detects unauthorized database row mutations: `get_evidence()` verifies hash and raises `EvidenceTamperingError` if payload deviates from seal.
* Implements payload deduplication: duplicate payloads return `is_new = False` without bloating storage.

### 3.3 Canonical Event Identity (`event_identity.py`)
Constructs deterministic canonical IDs independent of sensational or ephemeral headlines:
* Format: `EVENT_{TYPE}_{YYYYMMDD}_{AUTHORITY}_{DISCRIMINATOR}`
* Supported event types:
  * `MACRO_RATE_DECISION` (FOMC, ECB rate decisions)
  * `MACRO_INFLATION_PRINT` (CPI, PPI, PCE prints)
  * `MACRO_LABOR_PRINT` (NFP, Unemployment rate)
  * `REGULATORY_ENFORCEMENT` (SEC, CFTC, DOJ actions)
  * `REGULATORY_FILING` (8-K, 10-K, 10-Q corporate disclosures)
  * `ETF_FLOW_UPDATE` (Daily net flows)
  * `ONCHAIN_SUPPLY_EVENT` (Stablecoin mint/burn)
  * `ACADEMIC_PUBLICATION` (arXiv research preprints)
  * `GENERAL_MARKET_WIRE` (Syndicated news)

### 3.4 Entity Disambiguation Engine (`entity_resolver.py`)
Disambiguates cross-asset entities to prevent spurious correlations:
* **Crypto Spot Assets:** `BTC`, `ETH`, `SOL`
* **Public Equities:** `MSTR` (linked to BTC reserve), `COIN` (crypto exchange proxy)
* **ETF Products vs Issuers:** `IBIT` (ETF product) vs `BlackRock` (corporate issuer)
* **Stablecoin Tokens vs Issuers:** `USDT` (token) vs `Tether` (issuer), `USDC` (token) vs `Circle` (issuer)
* **Macro Indicators:** `CPI`, `NFP`

### 3.5 Conflict Ledger (`conflict_ledger.py`)
Tracks multi-source contradictions and empirical data revisions:
* Types: `CONTRADICTORY_REPORT`, `DATA_REVISION`, `TIMING_DISCREPANCY`, `ENTITY_AMBIGUITY`.
* Statuses: `UNRESOLVED`, `QUARANTINED`, `OFFICIAL_PREVAILED`, `CONSENSUS_RECONCILED`.
* Quarantine rule: Events with active unresolved conflicts are quarantined from automated research synthesis.
* Resolution workflow: Requires research rationale notes and updates resolution timestamps.

### 3.6 Domain Adapters & Institutional Politeness (`base_adapter.py` & `adapters/`)
* **Single User-Agent Header:**
  `CBT-Research-Scout/3.0 (+https://github.com/cloudshome/CBT; contact: security@cloudshome.local)`
* **Zero Evasion:** Strictly no user-agent rotation, proxy cycling, or anti-scraping bypass techniques.
* **Circuit Breakers:** Automatically trips upon 3 consecutive connection or HTTP errors; enforces minimum 300s cooldown.
* **Token-Bucket Throttling:** Restricts request frequency according to publisher terms and robots.txt.

### 3.7 Multi-Layer Quality Firewall (`quality_firewall.py`)
1. **Layer 1 (Source Quality):** Validates registered status, active flag, and minimum officiality threshold ($\ge 0.50$).
2. **Layer 2 (Evidence Integrity):** Validates 64-character SHA-256 seal, entity grounding ($\ge 1$ resolved entity), and ensures no active conflict ledger quarantine.
3. **Layer 3 (Point-in-Time Causality):** Enforces temporal precedence ($t_{\text{observed}} \ge t_{\text{published}}$ and $t_{\text{event}} \le t_{\text{observed}}$), preventing lookahead bias.

### 3.8 Scout Controller & Observability REST API (`scout_controller.py`, `routes.py`)
* `POST /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/run`: Triggers rate-limited crawl runs.
* `GET /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/status`: Returns current status, run counts, and invariants.
* `GET /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/sources`: Lists registered sources, officiality, and rate limits.
* `GET /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/runs/{run_id}`: Detailed telemetry for specific crawl run.
* `GET /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/evidence/{evidence_id}`: Retrieves raw unparsed evidence from vault.
* `GET /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/conflicts`: Queries active discrepancies and revisions.
* `GET /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/health`: Circuit breaker states and adapter health.
* `POST /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/pause`: Halts autonomous intelligence polling.
* `POST /api/v1/intelligence/crawler/resume`: Resumes autonomous intelligence polling.

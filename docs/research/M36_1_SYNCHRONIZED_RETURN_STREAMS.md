# M36.1 — Synchronized Return Streams & Calendar-Aligned Methodology

## Overview
Evaluating multi-strategy portfolio diversification in systematic quantitative research requires strict calendar alignment. Comparing strategies solely on trade logs introduces asynchronous bias, ignores idle cash periods, and fails to penalize high-drawdown dormant intervals.

Under **M36.1**, we reconstructed fully synchronized daily and monthly return streams across **1,754 calendar days** from **January 1, 2022 00:00 UTC** to **October 20, 2026 23:00 UTC**.

---

## 1. Methodology of Synchronization

### Calendar Alignment Rules
1. **Day Definition:** Fixed 24-hour UTC window from 00:00:00 to 23:59:59.
2. **Zero-Return Assignment:** On any calendar day where a strategy is not in a position or closes no trades, its daily net return is set to $R_{\text{day}} = 0.000\text{R}$.
3. **Multi-Day Hold Attribution:** For multi-day swing trades, mark-to-market daily returns are computed based on the close price of each UTC day, preventing lumpy end-of-trade PnL spikes.
4. **Intraday Compounding & Aggregation:** Multiple intraday fills within the same calendar day are algebraically summed in normalized risk space ($R$).

### Frictional & Execution Adjustments
Every trade reflected in the return matrix includes realistic conservative execution frictions:
- **Taker Fee:** $0.040\%$ ($4.0\text{ bps}$)
- **Maker Fee:** $0.020\%$ ($2.0\text{ bps}$)
- **Adverse Slippage:** $0.010\%$ ($1.0\text{ bps}$)
- **Bid-Ask Spread:** $0.010\%$ ($1.0\text{ bps}$)
- Total round-trip friction on market orders: $\approx 0.100\%$ ($10.0\text{ bps}$).
